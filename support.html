Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your SheValue subscription anytime through your Apple ID account settings on your iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > SheValue.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app and tap "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen. This will restore any active subscription linked to your Apple ID.
